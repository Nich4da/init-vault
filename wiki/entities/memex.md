# Memex

Vannevar Bush's 1945 vision of a personal, curated knowledge store with **associative
trails** linking documents — cited as the spiritual ancestor of the
[[persistent-wiki-pattern]] ([[llm-wiki-idea]]).

## Why it matters here
- Bush's vision was **private, actively curated**, with the connections between documents
  as valuable as the documents themselves — closer to this wiki than to what the web became.
- The unsolved part of his vision was **who does the maintenance** of those trails. In this
  pattern, the LLM is that answer — it maintains the links and summaries at near-zero cost.
